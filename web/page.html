Secure, memorable passphrases built from fantasy words made of English syllables, or from dictionary words, joined in CamelCase or with dashes, optionally ending in a symbol and two digits. Generated locally in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

Click a passphrase to copy it.

    Uses crypto.getRandomValues. Also available as a command-line script: github.com/adamburr/pw. Public domain. Word list from Alan Beale's 12dicts, also public domain.
